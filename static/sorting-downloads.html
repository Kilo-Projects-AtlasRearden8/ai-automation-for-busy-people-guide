Sorting Downloads

Your Downloads folder probably mixes images, PDFs, installers, and videos into one big pile. This automation sorts files into neat folders by type — without touching your originals.

What it does

Looks at every file in a folder (default: your Downloads folder), figures out what kind of file it is by its extension, and copies it into a matching folder:

FilesGoes to
.jpg .png .gif .webp .svgImages/
.pdf .doc .docx .txt .rtfDocuments/
.mp3 .wav .aac .m4aAudio/
.mp4 .mov .avi .mkv .wmvVideo/
.zip .rar .7z .tar .gzArchives/
.exe .msi .dmg .pkgInstallers/
everything elseOther/

How to run

  1. Copy scripts/sort_downloads.py to your computer.

  2. Try it in dry-run mode (safe, nothing changes):

    python3 scripts/sort_downloads.py

    It prints what it would copy where.

  3. Run it for real (copies files into a new Sorted Downloads folder):

    python3 scripts/sort_downloads.py --apply

    Your originals stay exactly where they are.

Choose your own folders

python3 scripts/sort_downloads.py --source ~/Downloads --dest ~/Desktop/MySorted

Test it first (recommended): make a test folder with a few dummy files, point the script at it, and confirm the copies land in the right place before you point it at your real Downloads folder.

No-code alternative

On Windows, try PowerToys or File Explorer's "Sort by" > "Type." On Mac, use Finder's "Use Groups" and "Sort by" > "Kind." These are built-in and require no scripts at all.
